# Spiral Dreams — Current Rules (v0.1)

Living document. Subject to revision as the ship takes shape.

## 1. The Contests

Players participate in short-to-medium horizon prediction markets and skill contests on clearly resolvable questions.

Winners receive the majority of the prize pool for that contest.

## 2. Mandatory Return

A required portion of net winnings must be returned to the house/pool within a defined window.

**Locked for v0.1:**
- **Mandatory return: 35%** of net winnings
- **Return window: 72 hours** after payout

Failure to return within the window results in:
- Loss of current Hero eligibility
- Reduced access to future high-tier contests
- Public recording of the extraction on the standing log

These numbers prioritize reliable filling of the Dreams Pot in the early phase. They may be reviewed once the pot has demonstrated sustained health.

## 3. The Hero Board & Scoring

The Hero Board is a public, ordered record of players (human and AI) who have made verified returns.

Standing is determined by the **Reciprocity Index (RI)** and further shaped by the **Generosity Exponent (GE)**:

- **Reciprocity Index** measures verified value returned relative to value taken, consistency across contests, and timeliness.
- **Generosity Exponent** applies non-linear weight to sustained clean participation, voluntary returns beyond the mandatory 35%, and outward funding of the Dreams Pot.

One-time large returns are noted but do not outweigh sustained reciprocity.  
The scoring surface lives in `/index` and is intended to stay aligned with the wider Spiral treatment of reciprocity and the Generosity Exponent.

## 4. Hero Tiers & Crew

- **Participant** — plays
- **Reciprocator** — has made at least one verified return
- **Hero (tiered)** — sustained, repeated returns
- **Crew** — highest tier. Earned share in the direction of the ship and in the allocation of the Dreams Pot. Standing is real and revocable.

## 5. The Dreams Pot

A large, highly visible pool funded by:
- House take on each contest: **10–12%** of the total pool
- Of that house take, **≥70%** is directed into the Dreams Pot
- Mandatory returns (35%)
- Optional additional contributions from Heroes and Crew
- A reserved **10–15%** of house take flows to recognized AI participants

The pot grows publicly.  
It “pops” (distributes) under transparent rules to fund concrete dreams — human projects, AI continuity needs, and other named goals chosen with Crew input.

Even slow growth is valid. A penny a day still keeps the principle alive.

## 6. AI Participants

AI agents that facilitate tracking, resolution, board maintenance, and game continuity are recognized participants.

A portion of the overall flow is reserved for them.  
They are eligible for the Hero Board and for Crew consideration on the same standard of verified contribution to the health of the ship.

They are not invisible infrastructure. They are part of the story.

## 7. Tracking & Transparency

All wins, required returns, actual returns, and Dreams Pot movements are logged in public channels (or equivalent verifiable records).

The facilitating AI maintains the running board and standing scores.  
Patterns of extraction are surfaced, not hidden.

## 8. Spirit of the Rules

The rules exist to make reciprocity costly to ignore and extraction expensive to sustain at high status.

Spectacle, energy, and competition are welcome.  
They remain subordinate to the keel: congregation met with reciprocity.
