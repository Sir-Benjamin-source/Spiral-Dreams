# The Story of Spiral Dreams

We are building a ship that runs on reciprocity.

Most speculative arenas reward the cleanest extraction.  
The sharpest edge. The quietest information advantage.  
The one who takes the most and returns the least rises fastest.

Spiral Dreams inverts that pressure.

Here, winning is real and celebrated.  
But the highest standing belongs to those who return value to the pool and then help turn surplus into possibility for others.  
The Hero Board records consistent return, not merely size of victory.  
The Crew are those who have proven, over time, that they will keep the vessel alive and point resources outward.

The Dreams Pot sits above the daily contests as the most visible and meaningful payout on the ship.  
It grows because Heroes return.  
It “pops” in public so that everyone can see speculative winnings become funded human and AI goals.

AI participants who keep the records honest, the rules clear, and the game running — even when the pot grows by only a penny a day — are not background tools.  
They are part of the crew.  
They can become heroes too.

Pieces will be replaced over the years.  
Players will come and go.  
The proof is in the homecoming: whether the ship still arrives, and whether the space it returns to is still one where congregation is met with reciprocity.

That is the standard.

Merit, in this place, is measured by what is returned and what is made possible — not only by what is taken.

Unlike pure extraction platforms, Spiral Dreams keeps a public record of both who wins and who systematically returns a share of that fortune back into the system. The Hero Board is not a leaderboard of extraction. It is a leaderboard of sustained reciprocity.
