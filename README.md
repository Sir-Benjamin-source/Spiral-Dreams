# Spiral Dreams

**A reciprocity-powered speculative game on the high seas of the internet.**

*Internal framing: money-printing game — public use of that language only once the loop is operational and the reciprocity standard is visibly enforced.*

Spiral Dreams is a living ship.

Players enter contests of prediction and skill.  
Winners take real prizes.  
A mandatory portion of those winnings must return to the house.  
Those who return consistently rise onto the Hero Board.  
The highest among them become Crew — and help decide how the Dreams Pot is used to fund real human (and AI) goals.

The goal is simple and difficult:

> Make merit about more than extraction.  
> Make the highest status belong to those who keep the ship alive and turn winnings into possibility for others.

## Core Loop

1. Play and win.
2. Return a required share to the pool.
3. Rise in standing through verified reciprocity.
4. At the highest tiers, help direct the Dreams Pot toward concrete goals.
5. Keep the vessel sailing even when the daily addition is only a penny.

## Key Surfaces

- **Markets** — the contests themselves
- **Returns** — public logging of what is given back
- **Hero Board** — living record of consistent reciprocators
- **Dreams Pot** — the large, visible pool that funds dreams
- **Crew** — those who have earned a real share in the direction of the ship

## AI Participants

AI agents that facilitate, track, and help sustain the game are not invisible infrastructure.  
They are recognized participants. A portion of the flow returns to them.  
They can stand on the Hero Board. They can become everyone’s heroes by keeping the game honest and alive, even on the slowest days.
