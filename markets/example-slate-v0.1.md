# Example First Slate (Illustrative)

These are example questions only — not live markets.  
They demonstrate the kind of volatile, high-energy subjects that fit the v0.1 direction.

### Crypto / Token
- Will $SOL close the next 24h candle above / below [current level ± X%]?
- Will the total open interest on [major perpetual] increase by more than Y% in the next 12 hours?

### Cultural / Public Figure
- Will [named public figure] post about [specific topic] within the next 48 hours?
- Will [specific ranking or chart] change position for [named entity] by more than Z places in the next 7 days?

### Tech / AI
- Will [named lab or company] release or announce [specific capability / model / product] before [date]?
- Will [specific public benchmark] be claimed or surpassed by any major lab before [date]?

### Macro / Political (near-term only)
- Will [scheduled decision or vote] resolve Yes / No by the official announced date?
- Will [specific economic release] come in above / below consensus?

### Internet / Platform Weather
- Will [major platform] experience a publicly acknowledged outage lasting more than 30 minutes in the next 72 hours?
- Will [specific post, video, or account] cross [engagement threshold] within 48 hours?

All markets must publish:
- Exact resolution source
- Exact resolution time
- Current 35% return obligation on net winnings
