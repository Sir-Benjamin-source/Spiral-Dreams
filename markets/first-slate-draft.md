# First Contest Slate — Draft (Crypto-First)

These are candidate questions for the initial micro-stakes / points loop.  
Final selection and exact resolution sources will be locked before any real value is at risk.

## Crypto (Priority)

1. Will Bitcoin close the next daily candle above / below a stated level (± defined band)?
2. Will the 24h percentage move of a named high-beta token exceed a stated threshold?
3. Will total open interest on a major perpetual market rise or fall by more than X% in the next 12 hours?

## Secondary Volatile

4. Will a named major AI lab or company make a public model / product announcement before a stated date?
5. Will a specific public platform or service experience a widely reported outage lasting more than 30 minutes in the next 72 hours?

All contests operate under the standing rules:
- 35% mandatory return of net winnings
- 72-hour return window
- Public logging of wins and returns
- Hero Board updated only on verified returns
